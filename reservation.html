Booking Policies

We request that any cancellations or modifications to your reservation be made no less than 24 hours prior to your scheduled booking time. Late cancellations may be subject to a nominal fee to account for reserved premium arrangements.
To ensure the highest quality of service, we hold tables for a maximum of 15 minutes past the reservation time. If you anticipate arriving late, please notify our concierge team immediately.
During peak dining hours, table allocations are scheduled for a duration of 2 hours for groups up to 4 guests, and 2.5 hours for larger groups. This allows us to accommodate all our esteemed guests effectively.
Aromatica enforces a smart casual dress code. Gentlemen are requested to avoid sleeveless shirts. We kindly ask all guests to refrain from flash photography in the main dining areas to maintain an intimate atmosphere.
